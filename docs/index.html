How it works

Each puzzle is a real TagPro map with a start, a starting velocity and a target. Touch the centre of the green cross as fast as you can, at any speed. The faded balls are bots that ran the same puzzle: the planning bot tries thousands of key presses ahead in the real physics, the simple bot just steers at the target, and the network bots learned to drive from the planning bot.

It's about the best time. Retry a puzzle as often as you like (R), then move on (N). Every puzzle has a WR, the fastest time by any player, and you can race the WR holder's run as the gold ghost. The leaderboard counts the WRs each player holds.

Modes sort puzzles by how hard the best time is to reach. At first that comes from the bots: on Easy, just steering at the target is about as fast as careful planning; on Expert, careful planning beats plain steering by 40% or more (or a bot couldn't finish at all). Once 3 players have finished a puzzle, its mode comes from how close players get to the record instead. Each mode keeps its own progress.

Medals: blue while you hold the WR (a tie counts; it replaces the other medal), gold for beating the planning bot, silver for finishing within 10% of it, bronze for finishing. Click your medals at the top to see your times against the WR on every puzzle. P pauses.

Rating works like chess, with each puzzle as an opponent, and counts when you move on. Your best time matching the planning bot's is a draw, and half its time is a win. Each try after the first takes 2% off, so retrying pays only when it really improves your time. Moving on without finishing counts as a loss. Every run is checked on the server by replaying your keys in the same physics.

Fair play: restarting quickly is fine. Accounts are flagged only for long streaks of restarts without pressing a movement key, or for restarting far faster than players normally do on that puzzle; flagged accounts are left out of the puzzle difficulty numbers and records until an admin reviews them. Resetting before you press a key doesn't cost rating.
